Forbidden Arcanus

In ATM10, Forbidden & Arcanus matters mostly for its Hephaestus Forge: it makes the Runic Star Altar controller, and so the road to the ATM Star. You need to take it to tier 5.

Checked against: Forbidden & Arcanus, branche 1.21.1 (pack : 2.6.1) + scripts KubeJS d'ATM10

  1. The forge's 5 tiers

The forge's 5 tiers

Each tier raises the maximum stock of the four essences (aureal, souls, blood, experience). A ritual that needs more than that stock can't start.

The rules

  • Tier 1: 1000 aureal, 10 souls, 10,000 blood, 900 XP.
  • Tier 2: 3000 aureal, 50 souls, 15,000 blood, 1350 XP.
  • Tier 3: 5000 aureal, 100 souls, 30,000 blood, 2500 XP.
  • Tier 4: 10,000 aureal, 500 souls, 50,000 blood, 5000 XP.
  • Tier 5: 20,000 aureal, 1000 souls, 100,000 blood, 7500 XP.

Sources: HephaestusForgeLevel.java

Upgrading the forge

Each upgrade is itself a forge ritual: a main item in the forge, ingredients on the pedestals, and essences.

The chain

  1. To tier 2Edelwood planks + 4 Arcane Crystals + 4 Spawner Scraps; 500 aureal, 6000 blood, 10 souls → tier 2 forge
  2. To tier 3chiseled polished darkstone + 4 Arcane Crystals + 4 Deorum ingots; 1000 aureal, 9000 blood, 50 souls → tier 3 forge
  3. To tier 4chiseled polished darkstone + 4 Stellarite Pieces + 4 Runes; 2000 aureal, 12,000 blood, 100 souls → tier 4 forge
  4. To tier 5Stellarite block + 4 Sculk Catalysts + 2 Dark Nether Stars + 2 Dragon Scales; 5000 aureal, 20,000 blood, 500 souls → tier 5 forge

Sources: hephaestus_forge/ritual/upgrade_tier_*.json

Duplicating relics (ATM10)

The Star Altar ritual needs 4 enhancers. ATM10 adds rituals to copy relics: the relic in the forge, 4 Obsidiansteel blocks and 4 ingots on the pedestals, tier 3 forge, 1000 aureal, 1000 blood, 100 souls and 100 XP. You get 2 relics back.

The rules

  • Artisan Relic: Deorum ingots.
  • Elementarium: Allthemodium ingots.
  • Crimson Stone: Vibranium ingots.
  • Divine Pact: Unobtainium ingots.

Sources: ATM10 kubejs/.../ForbiddenArcanus/Hephaestus_Forge.js
