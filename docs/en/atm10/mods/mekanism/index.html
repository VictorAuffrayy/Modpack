Mekanism

Mekanism is the mod everyone ends up building in ATM10: ore processing, reactors, then the MekaSuit. Multiblocks are where people get stuck, because the game only says "invalid structure". Here are the exact rules, read from the code that checks the structures, with a minimal working schematic for each.

Checked against: Mekanism 10.7.19 + config et scripts KubeJS d'ATM10 (branche main)

Uranium chain to fissile fuel

The fission reactor burns fissile fuel. It takes six steps, all in regular Mekanism machines.

The chain

  1. Enrichment Chamber1 uranium ingot → 2 Yellow Cake Uranium
  2. Chemical Oxidizer1 Yellow Cake Uranium → 250 mB Uranium Oxide
  3. Chemical Oxidizer, then Chemical Infuser ×2sulfur dust → sulfur dioxide; + oxygen → trioxide; + water vapor → Sulfuric Acid
  4. Chemical Dissolution Chamber1 fluorite gem + sulfuric acid (used up every tick) → 1000 mB Hydrofluoric Acid
  5. Chemical Infuser1 Hydrofluoric Acid + 1 Uranium Oxide → 2 Uranium Hexafluoride
  6. Isotopic Centrifuge1 Uranium Hexafluoride → 1 Fissile Fuel

Sources: Mekanism: recipe/processing/uranium/*.json

Fission reactor

The reactor heats water (or sodium) into steam, which you then send into a turbine. It doesn't make power on its own.

The rules

  • Size: any box from 3×3×3 to 18×18×18.
  • Edges: Fission Reactor Casing only. Faces: casing, glass, ports or logic adapter.
  • Inside: air, Fission Fuel Assemblies and Control Rod Assemblies, nothing else.
  • Each column of fuel assemblies must be unbroken, with exactly one control rod right on top. At least one column is required.
  • Each assembly burns at most 1 mB/t and stores 8000 mB of fuel (ATM10 config).
  • Above 1200 K the reactor takes damage. At 100% damage it can blow up: meltdowns are enabled in ATM10, with an 8-block radius.
  • Set the ports with the Configurator: fuel and coolant in, heated coolant out, waste out.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • Casing
  • FFission fuel assembly
  • GGlass (reactor or structural)
  • LLogic adapter
  • PPort / valve
  • RControl rod assembly

Sources: FissionReactorValidator.java · FissionReactorMultiblockData.java · ATM10 config/Mekanism/generators.toml

Industrial turbine

This is what turns steam from the reactor or boiler into power. The order of the layers matters.

The rules

  • Size: from 5×3×5 to 17×18×17. Length and width must be odd.
  • The Rotational Complex goes in the centre column. Below it, an unbroken stack of rotors down to the floor.
  • At the complex's level, a full layer of Pressure Dispersers fills the whole inside.
  • Electromagnetic Coils go above the complex and must connect to it (directly or coil to coil). Condensers too, above only.
  • Turbine Vents are not allowed below the complex's level.
  • You need 1 coil per 8 blades (ATM10 config), at most 2 blades per rotor and 28 blades in total.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • Casing
  • DPressure disperser
  • EElectromagnetic coil
  • ORotational complex
  • PPort / valve
  • TTurbine rotor (with blades)
  • VTurbine vent

Sources: TurbineValidator.java · ATM10 config/Mekanism/generators.toml

Thermoelectric boiler

The rules

  • Size: from 3×3×3 to 18×18×18.
  • Exactly one layer of Pressure Dispersers, wall to wall. Water sits below it, steam above.
  • At least one Superheating Element below the dispersers. If you place several, they must touch each other.
  • No isolated air pockets in the lower part.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • Casing
  • DPressure disperser
  • GGlass (reactor or structural)
  • HSuperheating element
  • PPort / valve

Sources: BoilerValidator.java

Thermal evaporation plant

Used for brine and then lithium. ATM10 changes the block recipe: 8 Thermal Evaporation Blocks from bronze, steel and a Superheating Element.

The rules

  • The base must be 4×4, height from 3 to 18 blocks.
  • The top stays open: the 4 centre blocks are empty, the corners may be missing.
  • Exactly one controller, on a face (not on an edge).

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • Casing
  • PPort / valve
  • XController

Sources: EvaporationValidator.java · ATM10 kubejs/.../Mekanism/Rebalance/Items.js

Induction matrix

The rules

  • Size: from 3×3×3 to 18×18×18.
  • Edges in Induction Casing; faces in casing, glass or Induction Ports.
  • Fill the inside with cells (storage) and providers (throughput), any tier, or air.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Casing
  • GGlass (reactor or structural)
  • IInduction cell / provider
  • PPort / valve

Sources: MatrixValidator.java

Nuclear waste: polonium and plutonium

ATM10 makes waste twice as useful as default Mekanism: 5 mB of waste instead of 10 for 1 mB of polonium or plutonium.

The chain

  1. Solar Neutron Activator5 mB Nuclear Waste → 1 mB Polonium
  2. Isotopic Centrifuge5 mB Nuclear Waste → 1 mB Plutonium
  3. Pressurized Reaction Chamber1000 mB Polonium + 1000 mB water + 1 fluorite dust → 1 Polonium Pellet (+ 1000 mB Spent Nuclear Waste)

Sources: ATM10 kubejs/.../Mekanism/Rebalance/Items.js · Mekanism: recipe/processing/lategame/*

SPS and antimatter

The Supercritical Phase Shifter turns polonium into antimatter. It's by far the hungriest multiblock in the mod.

One Antimatter Pellet (1000 mB antimatter in a Chemical Crystallizer) takes 1,000,000 mB of polonium, so 5,000,000 mB of waste in ATM10, and 100 GJ of energy.

The rules

  • Fixed size: 7×7×7, with cut corners and edges (see the schematic).
  • Each face's outline is SPS Casing; the face centres take casing or SPS Ports.
  • Supercharged Coils go inside, right behind a port. A coil that doesn't touch a port breaks the structure.
  • 1000 mB of polonium make 1 mB of antimatter, at 100,000 J per mB of polonium (ATM10 config).

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • Casing
  • KSupercharged coil
  • PPort / valve

Sources: SPSValidator.java · ATM10 config/Mekanism/general.toml

Fusion reactor

The rules

  • Fixed size: 5×5×5, plus-shaped faces (see the schematic).
  • The controller must sit in the centre of the top face.
  • The other face centres take frame, port, logic adapter or Laser Focus Matrix. The inside stays empty.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • Casing
  • MLaser focus matrix
  • PPort / valve
  • XController

Sources: FusionReactorValidator.java
