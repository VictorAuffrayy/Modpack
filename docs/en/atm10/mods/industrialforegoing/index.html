Industrial Foregoing

Industrial Foregoing automates farms, mobs and ores. Everything starts with plastic, and plastic starts with latex from trees.

Checked against: Industrial Foregoing, branche 1.21 (pack : 3.6.39)

Contents

  1. From latex to plastic

From latex to plastic

Latex is slow: place several Fluid Extractors around a tree farm rather than just one.

The chain

  1. Fluid Extractor (facing a log)1 oak log → 2 mB latex per operation (1% chance to strip the log)
  2. Latex Processing Unit750 mB latex + 500 mB water → 1 Dry Rubber
  3. Furnace1 Dry Rubber → 1 Plastic

Sources: recipe/fluid_extractor/oak.json · LatexProcessingUnitTile.java · recipe/plastic.json

Machine frames

The rules

  • The Simple Machine Frame is made in the Dissolution Chamber: 2 plastic, a Pity Machine Frame, 2 nether bricks, 2 iron ingots and a gold gear, with 250 mB latex.
  • Advanced and Supreme frames also go through the Dissolution Chamber: check their recipe in JEI/EMI.
  • Pink Slime Ingot: 2 iron + 2 gold ingots + 1000 mB pink slime, in the Dissolution Chamber.

Sources: dissolution_chamber/simple_machine_frame.json · dissolution_chamber/pink_slime_ingot.json

The laser drill

The rules

  • One Ore Laser Base (or Fluid Laser Base) and Laser Drills facing it, about 2 blocks away.
  • Each powered Laser Drill pushes the base's progress bar: the more drills, the faster it mines.
  • The base has 6 slots for coloured lenses to favour certain ores. Mining depth is shown in its interface.

Sources: LaserDrillTile.java · OreLaserBaseTile.java
