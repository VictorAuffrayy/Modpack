Mystical Agriculture

Mystical Agriculture grows resources. ATM10 changes several of its rules: seeds can only be made at the infusion altar, and the Allthemodium metals get their own tier, "Magical".

Checked against: Mystical Agriculture (branche 1.21 ; pack 8.0.28) + config, Mystical Customization et KubeJS d'ATM10

Tiering up essence

The chain

  1. Crafting table4 Inferium Essence in a plus + 1 Infusion Crystal in the middle → 1 Prudentium Essence
  2. Crafting tablesame shape with 4 Prudentium → 1 Tertium
  3. Crafting tablethen 4 Tertium → 1 Imperium, 4 Imperium → 1 Supremium → Supremium Essence

The rules

  • In ATM10 an Infusion Crystal lasts 500 uses. The Master Infusion Crystal never wears out.
  • Mobs don't drop Inferium in ATM10: it comes from the ore and from seeds.

Sources: recipe/prudentium_essence.json · ATM10 config/mysticalagriculture-common.toml

The infusion altar

The rules

  • 8 Infusion Pedestals around the altar, at the same height: 3 blocks away in the 4 directions, and 2 blocks away on the diagonals.
  • The centre item goes on the altar, the 8 ingredients on the pedestals. Start it with a redstone signal or a right-click with the Wand.
  • In ATM10 it's the only way to make seeds: crafting-table seed recipes are turned off.

The awakening altar

The rules

  • Same 8 spots as the infusion altar. The code accepts an Awakening Pedestal or an Essence Vessel on each; the schematic shows the usual layout (vessels on the sides, pedestals on the diagonals).
  • Like the infusion altar, start it with a redstone signal or a right-click with the Wand.
  • It makes Awakened Supremium, needed for the ATM Star and for Magical Soil.

ATM10's Magical tier

ATM10 adds Allthemodium, Vibranium and Unobtainium seeds (and a few others) in a custom tier 7, "Magical". Their seed uses a block of the metal, and they only grow on Magical Soil.

The rules

  • Magical Soil (crafting table, ATM10 recipe): Insanium block, 2x nether star block, 3x compressed dirt, Awakened Supremium Growth Accelerator, Insanium Farmland in the centre, dragon head, Piglich heart, 3x ender pearl block and a Moonlight Magic Crepe Myrtle sapling.
  • These crops can't be fertilised and never drop a second seed.

Sources: ATM10 mysticalcustomization/tiers/magical.json · ATM10 mysticalcustomization/crops/allthemodium.json · ATM10 kubejs/.../MysticalAgriculture/Recipes.js
