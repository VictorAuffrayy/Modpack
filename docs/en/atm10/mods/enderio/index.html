Ender IO

Ender IO is mostly about its alloys, conduits and a few unique machines. Beware of old guides: version 8 removed some alloys and changed recipes.

Alloys (Alloy Smelter)

Electrical Steel no longer exists in Ender IO 8. ATM10 adds SAG Mill recipes for ores, raw ores and ingots that had none.

The chain

  1. Conductive Alloyiron + copper → 2 ingots
  2. Redstone Alloyredstone + copper → 1 ingot
  3. Pulsating Alloyiron + ender pearl → 2 ingots
  4. Energetic Alloyredstone + Conductive Alloy + gold → 2 ingots
  5. Vibrant AlloyEnergetic Alloy + ender pearl + glowstone → 2 ingots
  6. Dark Steeliron + coal dust + obsidian → 2 ingots
  7. End Steelend stone + Dark Steel + obsidian → 2 ingots
  8. Soulariumsoul sand or soul soil + gold → 1 ingot

Sources: recipe/alloy_smelting/*.json · ATM10 kubejs/.../EnderIO/Recipes.js

The pack's two "Enchanters"

ATM10 has two blocks called Enchanter, and they do completely different things.

The rules

  • Ender IO's Enchanter: makes an enchanted book. It takes a book and quill (not a plain book), lapis and an item specific to the enchantment (a lily pad for Aqua Affinity, say), and costs XP levels.
  • Mystical Agriculture's Enchanter: enchants the item you put in directly, using ingredients and Experience Essence (Sharpness: 16 quartz + 64 Experience Essence).

Sources: Ender IO EnchanterBlockEntity / EnchanterRecipe · Ender IO recipe/enchanting/aqua_affinity.json · Mystical Agriculture recipe/enchanter/sharpness.json
