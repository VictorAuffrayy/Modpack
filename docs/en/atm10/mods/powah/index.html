Powah

Powah is the simplest early power source: few multiblocks, lots of tiers. The reactor and the energizing orb are the two pieces to understand.

Checked against: Powah 6.2.10 + config/powah.json5 d'ATM10

The reactor

The rules

  • It's 3 × 3 wide and 4 high: 36 reactor blocks of the same tier.
  • You need all 36 blocks in your inventory: place one and the reactor builds itself around it. The space must be clear.
  • It burns uraninite. Without carbon, output is divided by 1.2; without redstone, by 1.4. Redstone also raises fuel use and heat.
  • The hotter it runs, the less it makes. Liquid coolant: water. Solid coolant, from worst to best: snow, ice, packed ice, blue ice, then Powah's dry ice (−32).

Output per tier (ATM10)

The rules

  • Reactor: Starter 500, Basic 2000, Hardened 5000, Blazing 20,000, Niotic 50,000, Spirited 200,000, Nitro 500,000 FE/t.
  • These are maximums: real output depends on heat and additives (carbon, redstone).

Sources: ATM10 config/powah.json5

The energizing orb

The rules

  • Energizing Rods link themselves to the nearest orb within 4 blocks (ATM10 config), whichever way they face.
  • More rods = faster recipes. Each rod tier has its own rate: from 200 FE/t (Starter) to 400,000 FE/t (Nitro).
  • ATM10 changes some energizing recipes through its scripts: check JEI/EMI rather than an old guide.

Sources: ATM10 config/powah.json5 · ATM10 kubejs/.../Powah/Energizing.js · EnergizingRodBlock.setOrbPos
