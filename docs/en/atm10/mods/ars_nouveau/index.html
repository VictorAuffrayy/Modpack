Ars Nouveau

Ars Nouveau revolves around Source, the mod's magic energy. Most stalls come from a machine that can't find its pedestals or its Source: each machine has its own range.

Checked against: Ars Nouveau, branche 1.21.x (pack : 5.13.3) + config/ars_nouveau-common.toml d'ATM10

Contents

  1. Source

Source

The rules

  • A Source Jar holds 10,000 Source.
  • Machines draw from nearby jars; distance is counted as x + y + z steps, not a straight line.
  • In ATM10 you don't spawn with the book: you have to craft it.

Sources: SourceJarTile.java · SourceUtil.java · ATM10 config/ars_nouveau-common.toml

The Enchanting Apparatus

The rules

  • Arcane Pedestals must sit in a 7 × 7 × 7 cube centred on the apparatus (3 blocks in every direction, up and down included).
  • Source comes from jars within 10 blocks.

Sources: EnchantingApparatusTile.java · IPedestalMachine.pedestalList

The Imbuement Chamber

The rules

  • Its pedestals must touch it: a 3 × 3 × 3 cube around it.
  • It pulls Source from jars within 2 blocks, 200 at a time every second.
  • With no jar in range it still works, just very slowly: 10 Source per second.

Sources: ImbuementTile.java
