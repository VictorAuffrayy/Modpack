Occultism

Occultism is spirit summoning: draw a chalk pentacle, fill some bowls, and a spirit makes the item. One misplaced glyph and the ritual won't start, hence the schematics below, generated from the mod's own pentacles.

Checked against: Occultism 1.224.5 (tag release/v1.21.1-1.224.5) + données KubeJS d'ATM10

How a ritual works

The rules

  • The Golden Sacrificial Bowl goes in the centre of the pentacle. Ingredients go in regular Sacrificial Bowls, within 8 blocks of the centre bowl.
  • Start the ritual by right-clicking the centre bowl with the activation item (often a Book of Binding bound to a spirit).
  • Centre bowls have tiers: golden (1), iesnium (2), then the Eldritch and Celestial chalices (3).
  • Spirit Fire transforms what you throw in: a book becomes an empty Book of Binding, impure chalk becomes pure chalk, a flower becomes an Otherflower.

Sources: Ritual.SACRIFICIAL_BOWL_RANGE / GoldenSacrificialBowlBlockEntity · recipe/spirit_fire/*.json

Contact Eldritch Spirit and ATM10's miner
