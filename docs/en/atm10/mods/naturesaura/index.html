NaturesAura

Nature's Aura turns nature's aura into magic. Its first three structures (natural altar, tree ritual, offering table) are exact multiblocks: here they are as the mod checks them.

Checked against: Nature's Aura 41.10 (tag 41.10)

Contents

  1. The natural altar
  2. The tree ritual
  3. The offering table

The natural altar

The rules

  • 9 × 9 wide and 4 high: a floor of stone bricks, chiseled stone and planks, stone brick pillars and gold bricks on top.
  • Each material accepts variants through one of the mod's tags (other planks or other bricks work).
  • Empty cells in the schematic can hold anything.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • 0Nature altar (Nature's Aura)
  • BStone bricks (Minecraft)
  • Chiseled stone bricks (Minecraft)
  • MGold brick (Nature's Aura)
  • WOak planks (Minecraft)

Sources: Multiblocks.java (ALTAR)

The tree ritual

The rules

  • A sapling in the centre, a ring of Gold Powder around it, and 8 Wood Stands that hold the ingredients.
  • Any sapling (or log) used by a ritual recipe works in the centre.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • 0Oak sapling (Minecraft)
  • GGold powder (Nature's Aura)
  • WWood stand (Nature's Aura)

Sources: Multiblocks.java (TREE_RITUAL)

The offering table

The rules

  • Two rings of small flowers around the table (any small flower, not just the poppy shown).

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • 0Offering table (Nature's Aura)
  • RPoppy (Minecraft)

Sources: Multiblocks.java (OFFERING_TABLE)
