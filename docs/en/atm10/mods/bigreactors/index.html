Extreme Reactors

Extreme Reactors is the classic build-it-any-size reactor, and ATM10 makes it very profitable. Its build rules differ from Mekanism's, which is where people go wrong.

Checked against: Extreme Reactors 2.4.27 (code) + config/extremereactors/common.toml d'ATM10 (pack : 2.4.28)

ATM10 settings

The rules

  • Power output ×4 for the whole mod, then another ×3 for the reactor and ×3 for the turbine.
  • Fuel usage ×0.8.
  • Reinforced reactor: up to 32 blocks wide and 48 high. Reinforced turbine: 32 × 32.
  • Basic versions are capped: 5-block reactor, turbine 5 wide and 10 high.

Sources: ATM10 config/extremereactors/common.toml · ReactorVariant.java · TurbineVariant.java

The reactor

The rules

  • Frame (edges): Reactor Casing only.
  • You need at least one controller and at least one control rod.
  • All control rods on the same face. Behind each one, a straight line of Fuel Rods across the reactor, ending on a Reactor Casing (not glass).
  • No loose Fuel Rods: each one must belong to a column behind a control rod.
  • The rest of the inside: air or moderators (blocks and fluids the mod recognises).
  • Only one type of energy output per reactor.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • Casing
  • GGlass (reactor or structural)
  • NReactor control rod
  • PPort / valve
  • UReactor fuel rod
  • XController

Sources: MultiblockReactor.java

The turbine

The rules

  • Exactly one Rotor Bearing, on a face, and at least one controller.
  • The Rotor Shaft starts at the bearing and runs the whole interior until it hits the housing on the other side.
  • Blades extend continuously from the shaft and must be closer to the bearing than any metal block.
  • Metal blocks (the coil) form full or partial rings around the shaft, on the side away from the bearing.
  • Nothing else inside: shaft, blades, metal or air.

Minimal schematic (top view, layer by layer)

  • Air / empty
  • ARotor shaft
  • BRotor blade
  • Casing
  • PPort / valve
  • WMetal block (induction coil), e.g. gold
  • XController
  • ZRotor bearing

Sources: MultiblockTurbine.java
