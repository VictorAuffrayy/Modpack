The ATM Star is ATM10's final goal. In this version of the pack it's no longer crafted at a table: everything goes through the Runic Star Altar, a multiblock added by the ATM team.

The Runic Star Altar

The rules

  • It's an electric multiblock (Modern Industrialization): each recipe draws 2048 EU/t for 200 ticks.
  • The structure is 15 × 15 wide and 8 high, made of Forbidden & Arcanus blocks (darkstone, runes, arcane crystals, pedestals, Utrem jar…).
  • Hatches (item input, item output, energy) replace the darkstone blocks marked "h" on the edges.
  • The controller comes from a Hephaestus Forge ritual (tier 5): polished darkstone in the centre; around it an Unrealium block (or the Lunar Monstrosity loot bag), 2 Z-Logic Controllers, 2 Enderium gears, 2 Mundabitur Dust and a Quantum Injector; 5000 aureal, 5000 blood, 100 souls and 2500 XP; 4 enhancers (Artisan Relic, Elementarium, Divine Pact, Maledictus Pact).

The ATM Star recipe

Two Star Altar recipes make the ATM Star. The first is the big recipe; the second goes through shards.

The rules

  • Main recipe: 28 Unobtainium-Allthemodium alloy blocks, 15 3x compressed nether star blocks, 2 "awakened" Unobtainium-Vibranium alloy blocks, plus one each of: Oblivion Shard, Creative Essence, Nexium Emitter, Wither's Compass, Improbable Probability Device, Dragon Soul, Philosopher's Fuel, Pulsating Black Hole, Dimensional Seed and Patrick Star.
  • "Awakened" alloy block (also at the Star Altar): 1 Unobtainium-Vibranium alloy block + 4 Awakened Supremium Essence + 4 Awakened Supremium Gemstones + 4 Unbreaking I books.
  • Patrick Star: 13 magenta concrete, 11 pink concrete, 8 green concrete, 8 green concrete powder, 8 magenta concrete powder, 5 pink concrete powder and 2 lime concrete. There's also a Create Mechanical Crafter recipe.

Sources: ATM10 runic_multis/recipes/star_altar.js · ATM10 modpack/atm_star.js

The shard route

The chain

  1. Fragments 1 to 5 (Star Altar)48 ATM Star Shards + Soul Binding Crystal (fragment 1); 52 shards + a prism for the others: Whirlwind, Smelter, Sea, Terrastomp → 1 fragment each
  2. Infused Patrick Star (Star Altar)36 shards + 12 Infused Breath + 4 Mending I books + the 3 alloy ingots + Patrick Star → Infused Patrick Star
  3. ATM Star (Star Altar)the 5 fragments + Lens of the Killer + Mundabitur Dust + Corrupti Dust + Infused Patrick Star → 1 ATM Star
